## Methodology
Built upon the **AI Samson Character Consistency & Video Production Methodology**:
1. **Isolated Garment Pre-Pass**: Lock 4-angle dress turnaround in 3D isolation before compositing.
2. **Zero-Drift Try-On**: Preserve identity, facial features, hair, jewelry, and native studio environment 100%.
3. **Dual-Scale Master Contact Sheet**: Side-by-side full silhouette and close-up detail anchor.
4. **Editorial Catalogue Poses**: 5 high-fashion poses capturing movement and fabric flow.
5. **Turnaround & Video Commercial**: Keyframed Veo 3.1 video turntable and stitched 25s commercial reel.
