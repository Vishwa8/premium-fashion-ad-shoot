# AYURTI LUXURY PRODUCT CATALOG DESCRIPTION

## 1. Product Title
The Regal Kalibherva Embellished Maroon Anarkali Gown with Antique Zari & Tassels

## 2. Luxury Intro Paragraph
Exuding timeless regal opulence and contemporary couture grace, this breathtaking floor-length Anarkali gown in rich imperial maroon captures the zenith of festive glamour. Sculpted from premium heavy georgette that cascades with effortless fluidity, the gown is meticulously embellished with antique golden zari threadwork and glistening micro-sequins across the neckline and cuffs. Paired with a dramatic 16-panel flared hem and signature rear latkan tassels, it embodies the majestic spirit of royal celebration.

## 3. Design Philosophy
Harmonizing traditional Rajasthani craftsmanship with modern architectural tailoring, this piece focuses on silhouette integrity and radiant light reflection. The structured princess bodice flatters the natural torso, while the calibrated flare of the kalis generates cinematic movement with every stride. Delicate dori cords and handcrafted latkans celebrate the artisanal heritage of heirloom Indian couture.

## 4. Fabric Story
Crafted from high-density faux georgette, the fabric provides an exquisitely textured hand feel combined with an airy, billowing drape. Lined with silky breathable micro-crepe, the gown ensures absolute comfort and opaque structure. The deep maroon palette absorbs ambient warmth while allowing the golden zari motifs to shimmer with understated luxury.
